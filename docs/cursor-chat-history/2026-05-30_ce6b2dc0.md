## Örnek SQL (kullanım — belirli gün)

```sql
select
  user_id,
  count(*)::int as sessions_that_day,
  sum(coalesce(duration_seconds, 0))::int as total_seconds
from public.app_usage_sessions
where (started_at at time zone 'Europe/Istanbul')::date = date '2026-05-30'
group by user_id;
```
